My story

I've spent about 27 years in journalism, digital media and entrepreneurship. From Milliyet.com.tr to Ströer Media, from Rat Digital — the digital agency I founded — to publishing, I've always been after a good story.

I founded Guidy because I believe a trip, like a well-told story, should be shaped around the person. I plan your route by listening to you, book it with the backing of a licensed agency, and stay with you until the journey ends.
